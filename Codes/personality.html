My Opinion on using AI as a Tool, Not a Crutch:

LLMs such as ChatGPT, Claude, and CoPilot must be used as a tool not a crutch.

The many priorities for improvements to the way students are learning are still partially unmet argues Cardona et al. (2023). As such, safe, effective, and scalable technological approaches that address these priorities have been sought through Large language models (LLMs), such as ChatGPT, Claude and Copilot. In fact, these LLMs are rapidly becoming one of the most useful tools for students (Leonard, 2026). However instead of utilizing these tools to support learning students rather employ them to replace their own thinking, a trend I find concerning.

As a student myself, I utilize AI to assist me in understanding difficult topics and technical terms. At times, I would prompt the LLM to suggest examples that explain unfamiliar concepts such as how HTML and CSS works together in web programming and even to identify mistakes in my assignments. Nonetheless, full dependence on these LLM tools not only prevents students from developing their own skills, but also stifles critical thinking and problem-solving whilst promoting intellectual complacency.

In essence, AI should be used responsibly and viewed as a tool that can improve learning when used to make queries, practise skills and receive guidance (UNESCO, 2023) . As it happens, the ultimate goal should really be to develop into an independent learner who uses technology responsibly instead of relying on it completely.
