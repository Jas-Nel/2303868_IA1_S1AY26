CHAPTER 1: The Seven Cures for a Lean Purse

In fact, the first chapter: The Seven Cures for a Lean Purse speaks to how budgeting and saving by means of controlling unnecessaryy spending is the first step to financial stability, especially as a university student. In my case, when I receive my allowance from my parents, I could firstly put a portion of the money aside for savings whilst prioritizing essential expenses such as food for meal prepping and avoiding impluse purchases such as an outfit for every occasion. Likewise investing in ones education and technical skills to improve future earning potential is what also shatters the chains of financial hardship (Clason, 2018). The author went on further to note that small, consistent habits are what leads to sustained financial independece.

CHAPTER 2: The Five Laws of Gold

The second chapter: The Five Laws of Gold similarly speaks to how important wise monetary management, growth and protection is. Though the careful savings and investing of money accounts for its growth, this money can easily be lost through poor decision making and financial illiteracy. In my opinion, these core principles can be applied to everyday life by ensuring that financial opportunities and potential risks are thoroughly researched upon jumping into these investments whilst seeking advice from persons knowledgeable in the field to gain a better understanding of what it is that one may be getting themselves into and avoiding schemes that promise quick profits. It is through these lessons that I will become more financially responsible and make better decisions as I work toward independence.

Lessons Learnt

To conclude, it can be said that one doesn't have to wait until they have acquired a high paying job to start developing good financial habits. Regulaar savings, proper expense management, setting financial goals and invester in greater education are all key aspects of managing and safegaurding wealth to further achieve financial success. By applying these lessons throughout life, a strong financial foundation can be built, whilst working toward achieviing future goals and gaining greater independence.
